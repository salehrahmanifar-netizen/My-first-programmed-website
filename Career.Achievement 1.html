Unit 1: Using the Internet

This section explains how the internet is used in everyday life and how users interact with online systems. In this unit, I studied topics such as search engines, online services, email, virtual learning environments (VLE), and online safety. I learned how search engines use keywords, web crawlers, and algorithms to find and rank the most relevant information for users.

I also studied different types of online services, such as email, cloud storage, and social media, and how they allow users to communicate, share data, and access information from anywhere. In addition, I learned about VLEs, which are online platforms used by students and teachers to share resources and communicate.

Another important part of this unit was online safety. I learned about risks such as phishing, malware, and data theft, and how users can protect themselves by using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links, and keeping personal information secure. This section shows my understanding of how the internet works, how it is used in real life, and how to stay safe while using it.

Key unit topics

  • Internet usage in daily life
  • Search engines, keywords, and algorithms
  • Email, cloud storage, and social media
  • Virtual Learning Environments (VLE)
  • Online safety: phishing, malware, and data protection

Unit 2: Technology Systems

This section explains how technology systems work inside digital devices and how data is processed and transferred. In this unit, I studied topics such as mobile processors, operating systems, and different methods of data transmission. I learned that mobile processors act as the brain of a device, carrying out instructions and processing data, while operating systems manage hardware, run applications, and provide a user interface.

I also studied serial and parallel transmission, which explain how data is sent between devices. Serial transmission sends data one bit at a time and is reliable over long distances, while parallel transmission sends multiple bits at the same time and is faster over short distances. In addition, I learned about data structures such as stack and queue, which are used to organise and manage data efficiently.

This section also helped me understand how different components work together in real-world systems, such as smartphones and computers, to process, store, and transfer data. It shows my understanding of how technology systems operate and how they are used in everyday life.

Key unit topics

  • Mobile processors as device brains
  • Operating systems and user interfaces
  • Serial vs parallel data transmission
  • Data structures: stack and queue
  • Component interaction in smartphones and computers
